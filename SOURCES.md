### Verified Milestones & Quantitative Telemetry

#### 1990 — Hubble Space Telescope Deployment: The Flawed Eye (`hubble-launch-1990`)
- **Exact Date / Epoch**: April 24–25, 1990
- **Participating Agencies**: NASA, ESA
- **Location / Orbital Regime**: Low Earth Orbit (547 km Altitude / 28.5° Inclination)
- **Voyager 1 Distance at Event**: `42 AU` | **Target Distance from Earth**: `547 km`
- **Verified Key Statistics**:
  - **Primary Mirror Diameter**: `2.4 meters (7.9 feet)` — Primary optical collector ground from Corning Ultra-Low Expansion glass and coated with 65 nm of aluminum and 25 nm of magnesium fluoride
  - **Spherical Aberration Depth**: `~2.2 micrometers (1/50th human hair thickness)` — Polished too flat at the outer perimeter due to a 1.3 mm spacing error in the reflective null corrector test rig
  - **Operational Orbit Altitude**: `~540 to 612 km (Low Earth Orbit)` — Deployed by STS-31 Discovery at 28.5-degree inclination, orbiting Earth every ~95 minutes
  - **International Partnership Share**: `ESA ~15% institutional contribution` — ESA contributed the Faint Object Camera (FOC), the original solar arrays, and operational staffing at STScI in exchange for 15% observation time
  - **Launch Vehicle & Mission**: `Space Shuttle Discovery (STS-31)` — Launched from Kennedy Space Center LC-39B carrying a 5-astronaut crew and the 11,110 kg observatory in its payload bay
- **Primary Mission Citations**:
  - [Hubble's Mirror Flaw](https://science.nasa.gov/mission/hubble/observatory/design/optics/hubbles-mirror-flaw/) — *NASA Science* (2026-10-14)
  - [Servicing Mission 1 (SM1)](https://science.nasa.gov/mission/hubble/observatory/missions-to-hubble/servicing-mission-1/) — *NASA Science* (2026-10-14)

#### 1993 — Hubble Servicing Mission 1: Restoring Cosmic Sight (`hubble-repair-1993`)
- **Exact Date / Epoch**: December 2–13, 1993
- **Participating Agencies**: NASA, ESA
- **Location / Orbital Regime**: Low Earth Orbit (595 km Altitude / STS-61 Servicing Rendezvous)
- **Voyager 1 Distance at Event**: `54 AU` | **Target Distance from Earth**: `595 km`
- **Verified Key Statistics**:
  - **Spacewalk Duration**: `35 hours 28 minutes (5 EVAs)` — Five consecutive spacewalks conducted by two alternating teams (Musgrave/Hoffman and Thornton/Akers)
  - **Corrective Optics Installed**: `COSTAR & WFPC2 (with internal correction)` — Deployed ten motorized corrective mirrors into the optical paths of FOC, FOS, and GHRS
  - **Solar Arrays Replaced**: `2 Solar Arrays & Drive Electronics` — Replaced with rigidized arrays built by ESA to eliminate orbital thermal oscillations and spacecraft jitter
  - **Attitude Control Servicing**: `2 Rate Sensor Units (4 gyroscopes)` — Installed two new Rate Sensor Units containing 4 replacement gyroscopes to restore 3-axis fine pointing stability
  - **Hubble Deep Field Milestone**: `342 exposures / ~3,000 galaxies` — Dec 18–28, 1995: 342 exposures across 10 days in Ursa Major revealed ~3,000 primordial galaxies across a keyhole sky area
- **Primary Mission Citations**:
